Faire Tickets

Ticket Info

All Pennsylvanis Renaissance Faire tickets are date-specific and must be purchased in advance, tickets will not be sold at the gate. The gates open at 11:00am and close at 8:00pm on Saturdays and Sundays from August 15th through October 25th. Merchant booths close at 7:00pm. Children ages 4 and under do not need a ticket. The Faire is rain or shine, and all tickets are non-refundable.

Sunset Ticket Admission

Sunset admission tickets are released online each Faire day at 3:00pm. Sunset admission open at 4:00pm, and guests are free to enjoy evening performances, merchants, and more until the Faire closes at 8:00pm (merchant booths close at 7:00pm). Sunset tickets are valid only on the date of purchase and are not sold in advance.
