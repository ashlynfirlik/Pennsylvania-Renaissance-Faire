Faire Tickets

Regular Admission

All Pennsylvania Renaissance Faire tickets are date-specific and must be purchased in advance, tickets will not be sold at the gate. The gates open at 11:00am and close at 8:00pm on Saturdays and Sundays from August 15th through October 25th. Merchant booths close at 7:00pm. Children ages 4 and under do not need a ticket. The Faire is rain or shine, and all tickets are non-refundable.

Sunset Ticket Admission

Sunset admission tickets are released online each Faire day at 3:00pm. Sunset admission opens at 4:00pm, and guests are free to enjoy evening performances, merchants, and more until the Faire closes at 8:00pm (merchant booths close at 7:00pm). Sunset tickets are valid only on the date of purchase and are not sold in advance.

Book a Workshop

About our Workshops

Learn traditional crafts from The Shire's master artisans. Each Faire day, guests can attend glassblowing, archery, and pottery workshops individually or in small groups. Workshops are held in 1.5 hour time slots at 12:00pm, 2:00pm, 4:00pm, and 6:00pm. All tools and materials are provided, and you will take home your finished piece as a keepsake.

Because each workshop is taught in small groups for the best experience, it is highly recommended to purchase your workshop tickets in advance. All workshop participants must also hold a valid Faire ticket.
