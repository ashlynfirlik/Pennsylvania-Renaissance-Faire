Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to our most frequently asked questions here!

Are tickets sold at the gate?

No. Tickets must be purchased in advance online, and all sales are date-specific, non-transferable, and non-refundable.

What are the Faire's hours?

Gates open at 11:00am and close at 8:00pm on Saturdays and Sundays from August 15th through October 25th. Sunset admission opens at 4:00pm, and merchant booths close at 7:00pm.

Does the Faire close if it rains?

No. The Faire is a rain or shine event, so guests are encouraged to dress accordingly and enjoy regardless of the weather!

Can I leave and re-enter on the same day?

Yes! Same day re-entry is permitted. Visit the exit gate attendant before leaving to recevie a hand stamp that will allow you re-entry.

Are costumes required?

No. While costumes are welcome and encouraged, guests attend in whatever clothing they feel comfortable.

Can I bring outside food and drinks?

No. Outside food and drinks are not permitted inside the Faire grounds, with the exception of baby food or medically necessary dietary foods.

Is the Faire wheelchair accessible?

Yes! The Shire's main lanes are completely wheelchair and stroller accessible, and accessible parking is available near the Main entrance (see directions page for more info).

Do I need to bring cash?

Card and tap-to-pay are accepted by every merchant, though cash is helpful for tipping performers. ATMs are available on site.

Can I take photos/videos?

Yes! Photograhpy and recording are welcome throughout The Shire and during shows. We kindly ask that guests avoid flash photography during shows to respect the performers.
