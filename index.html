About

Welcome to The Shire in Mount Hope, where we welcome you to enjoy the whimsy and excitement of the Pennsylvania Renaissance Faire. Across 35 acres, you will find talented merchants, skilled performers, brave knights jousting in front of the Queen's Court, and much more. You can also book hands-on workshops and demos that will immerse you in the skills of the era. The Pennsylvania Renaissance Faire holds enough magic for everyone.
